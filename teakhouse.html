FAQ

The honest answers.

Which hotels is it for?
Hotels from 3 to 5 stars that want guests to book direct. The demo is dressed as a five-star riverside hotel; the same system is tailored to your rooms, your photographs and your rate.
What is the difference between Teak House and Teak House Plus?
Teak House (฿119,000) covers rooms, offers, the booking dock, the concierge, the owner panel, 16 languages and 13 currencies. Teak House Plus (฿189,000) adds dining and event spaces, for hotels with restaurants and function rooms.
Which languages and currencies?
16 languages: English, Thai, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Vietnamese and Malay. 13 currencies: THB, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CNY, SGD, AUD, HKD, KRW, CHF, AED, INR. The owner panel runs in English and Thai.
Can I edit rooms, offers, menus and events myself?
Yes. Rooms, prices, photographs, offers, the menu and the events calendar are all edited from the owner panel. No developer, no ticket, no waiting.
Do I pay monthly?
No monthly software fee. You own the system. The only ongoing costs are your own domain and hosting, with optional studio maintenance.
How long to launch?
Most properties launch within a few weeks, depending on how ready the photographs, room details and menus are. The studio handles the build, content, setup and owner training.
Does it sync with Booking.com?
No. This is a direct-booking system built to move guests to your own site and keep the 15 to 20% commission in the property. There is no channel manager or OTA sync.
