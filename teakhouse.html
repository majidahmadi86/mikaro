FAQ

The honest answers.

What is the platform built on?
The platform is built on Next.js and React with TypeScript, styled with Tailwind, and data handled through Prisma on Postgres. It runs on modern, well-supported foundations and deploys cleanly to Vercel.
Who owns the site and the data?
You do. It is your property's site and your database, with your guest and booking data. The studio builds, tailors and maintains the platform for you, and you are never locked to a third-party booking marketplace.
Can it run in more than one language?
Yes. It is bilingual by default, with English and Thai in the live demo, and more locales added as your property needs them. Pricing and display are multi-currency too.
What does a deployment include?
A build tailored to your property: your branding, rooms, rates and copy, with the booking engine, owner dashboard, AI concierge and guest accounts wired end to end. The studio sets it up, launches it and keeps it maintained.
Does it sync with Booking.com or Agoda?
No. The Teak House Platform is a direct-booking system built to keep OTA commission in the house. There is no channel manager and no OTA sync. It is designed to grow your direct bookings, not to manage marketplace listings.
