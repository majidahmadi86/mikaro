FAQ

The honest answers.

Can I edit rooms, menus and events myself?
Yes, all three. The rooms manager handles photos, prices, amenities and details; the dining manager handles categories, dishes, prices and dish photos; the events manager handles dates, descriptions and seat counts. No developer, no ticket, no waiting.
Can I change the photos myself?
Yes. Owner image uploads are built into the panel: room photos, dish photos, the dining hero, the events hero. You upload, the site updates. Nothing goes through the studio unless you want it to.
How does the pricing calendar work?
Each room has a base rate. On top of that you add seasonal ranges (high season, weekend premium) and specific-date overrides (Songkran, New Year). A date override beats a season, a season beats the base rate. Guests see the price for each night, and the booking adds the stay up exactly.
Does it take table bookings for the restaurant?
Yes, and you control it with one switch. Turn table reservations on, set your service hours, sittings per day and largest party, and guests can request a table from the dining page. Requests land in the owner reservations manager. No card details are taken · the house confirms by phone or LINE.
What about events in the pavilion?
There is a function-space page for weddings, private dinners and parties, and a special events calendar for the evenings you run yourself. Guests request seats through a short form, and every request appears in the owner events manager.
What languages?
English and Thai, written natively, with a one-tap switch for guests. That includes the menu, the events and the confirmation emails · not just the headline pages. The owner panel switches language too. More locales can be added for your property. Prices show in THB, USD and EUR.
Do I pay monthly?
No monthly software fee. You own the system. It is a one-time build; the only ongoing costs are your own domain and hosting, with optional studio maintenance if you want it.
How long to launch?
Most properties launch within a few weeks, depending on how ready your photos, room details and menu are. The studio handles the build, content, setup and owner training.
Does it sync with Booking.com?
No, and that is the point. Booking.com and Agoda take 15 to 20% of every booking. This is a direct-booking system built to move guests to your own site and keep that commission in the property. There is no channel manager or OTA sync.
