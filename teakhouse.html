FAQ

The honest answers.

Can I edit rooms myself?
Yes. The rooms manager lets you edit photos, prices, amenities and details yourself, no developer needed. Seasonal pricing rules and availability are yours to change too.
What languages?
English and Thai, written natively, with a one-tap switch for guests. More locales can be added for your property. Prices show in THB, USD and EUR.
Do I pay monthly?
No monthly software fee. You own the system. It is a one-time build; the only ongoing costs are your own domain and hosting, with optional studio maintenance if you want it.
How long to launch?
Most properties launch within a few weeks, depending on how ready your photos and room details are. The studio handles the build, content, setup and owner training.
Does it sync with Booking.com?
No, and that is the point. Booking.com and Agoda take 15-20% of every booking. This is a direct-booking system built to move guests to your own site and keep that commission in the property. There is no channel manager or OTA sync.
