FAQ

Before the first conversation.

Who is Solenne for?
Ultra-luxury hotels and hotel groups: a single exceptional property, or a collection of estates that should be reserved, experienced and managed as one.
Can each estate keep its own character?
Yes. Each estate has its own residences, tables, spaces, photography and writing. The collection gives them one standard and one reservation.
Which languages and currencies?
16 languages: English, Thai, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Vietnamese and Malay. 13 currencies: THB, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CNY, SGD, AUD, HKD, KRW, CHF, AED, INR. The owner panel runs in English and Thai.
How is it priced?
Solenne is priced per property, on request. Write to the studio on LINE and we will arrange a private walkthrough.
Can travel advisors work with it?
Yes. The travel-partner portal lets agencies compose whole journeys, send them as one proposal and see their commission on every part of the stay.
