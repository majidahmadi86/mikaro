Three houses, one voice.

A bilingual corporate hub for Groupe Balzac, uniting a French cultural café in Bangkok, an antiques house and a real estate brand. Our second project for the same client, after balzacantiques.ch.

Single-column editorial stack on mobile, with the franchise form one tap away.

Client

Groupe Balzac

Timeline

2 weeks · 9 to 23 September 2026

Languages

English · French, full parity

Hosting

Client's own VPS

Pages

Home, About, Vision, Franchise, Contact, Legal, Privacy

The brief

Groupe Balzac needed one place to present its houses together and open the door to franchise partners abroad, in English and French with full parity.

The design

An editorial full-bleed layout in navy, cream, forest green and antique gold, with a typeset plaque logo and self-hosted EB Garamond throughout.

The build

A route-based bilingual structure with seven pages in each language, franchise enquiry and contact forms with email delivery and captcha protection, hosted on the client's own VPS.

The result

Lighthouse on production, mobile and desktop: 100 Performance · 100 Accessibility · 100 Best Practices · 100 SEO.
