Case study · Client work · Live

A Bangkok antiques house, open to the world.

A bilingual online store for Balzac Antiques, selling watches, books, art and design pieces to collectors in Switzerland, France and Thailand.

On mobile, every piece is one tap from the cart.

Client

Balzac Antiques, Bangkok

Timeline

3 weeks · 9 to 31 July 2026

Languages

English · French, route-based with full parity

Currencies

EUR · USD · CHF display, card checkout in EUR

Hosting

Client's own VPS

The brief

Balzac Antiques needed a real store in English and French, not a catalogue, that the owner could run on his own.

The design

A quiet gallery feel in cream and antique gold, a homepage slideshow the owner curates, category covers that fill themselves from the newest pieces, and a framed zoom view for every photograph.

The build

Add to cart from any page with a slide-out drawer, guest checkout with optional accounts and order history, Stripe card payments in euros, and pieces that reserve and mark themselves sold so nothing is ever sold twice. Behind it, an admin panel for pieces, photos, orders and enquiries, plus contact, sell-to-us and newsletter forms with email delivery.
