FAQ

The honest answers.

Can I edit treatments and prices myself?
Yes. The treatments manager holds the name, category, from-price, duration, downtime, photo and publish state. Change a price at 09:00 and the site and the booking flow both show it at 09:00. No developer, no ticket.
How does the deposit work?
A patient picks a treatment, a date and a time, then pays a ฿500 deposit that holds the slot. It is not a fee · it comes off the treatment on the day. Rescheduling is free up to 24 hours before. The amount is a setting, so it can be ฿300 or ฿1,000 for your clinic.
What if a patient cancels or does not turn up?
Reception marks the appointment on the dashboard and the slot reopens. The panel tracks actual no-shows next to no-shows prevented, so you can see what the deposit policy is doing rather than guess at it. Your own refund rules are yours to set and state on the page.
Can I close a day or a single time?
Yes. In the calendar, click a day to close it or a slot to close that time. Closed days and slots disappear from the public booking grid immediately, so nobody books an hour you are not there.
Does it connect to our HIS or EMR?
No, and we say so up front. PRAOW is the front of the clinic: the website, the booking, the deposits, the schedule and the client list. Clinical records, e-prescribing, insurance claims and pharmacy stock stay in whatever system you already use.
What languages?
English and Thai, written natively, with a one-tap switch. That covers the treatment pages, the booking flow, the confirmation and the AI receptionist. The clinic panel switches language too, so Thai-speaking reception is not working in English all day.
Do I pay monthly?
No monthly software fee. You own the system. It is a one-time build; the only ongoing costs are your own domain and hosting, with optional studio maintenance if you want it.
How long to launch?
Most clinics launch within a few weeks, depending on how ready your treatment list, prices and before and after sets are. The studio handles the build, the content, the setup and the training for reception.
